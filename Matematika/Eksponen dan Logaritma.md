# Logaritma

> [!info] Notasi Logaritma
> $$ a^b = c, $$
> 
> $$ \log_a(c) = b $$

Aturan-aturan:

- $\log(ab) = \log(a) + \log(b)$
- $\log_{a^m}(b^n) = \frac{n}{m} \log_a b$
- $\frac{1}{\log_a b} = \log_b a$
- $\log_m a = \frac{\log_b a}{\log_b m}$

Aturan pencoretan:

- $\log_m 1 = 0$
- $\log_m m = 1$
- $\log_m m^p = p$
- $m^{\log_m a} = a$
