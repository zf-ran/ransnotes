While recount text is generally divided into two types, personal and historical, we are learning about historical in this chapter.

# Purpose

The purpose of historical recount text is **to retell, recount, and inform**.

# Structure

- **Orientation**  
	Orientation introduces the reader to the event. Like the definition, origin, etc.
- **Events**  
	The chronological events that happens in the topic.
- **Reorientation**  
	Can contain comment and/or conclusion.

# Language Features

- Simple past tense.
- Temporal conjunction.
