# How to Charge Your Phone

This manual is for a phone using USB C port, a cable USB A to USB C, and a USB A adaptor.

![[USB Cables.jpg]]

## Preparations

1. Charging cable and an adaptor.
2. A working and active electric socket.
3. A phone to charge.

## Steps

1. Make sure your adaptor is compatible with the socket.
2. And also make sure you have an electricity.
3. Before you plug the adaptor, make sure your hands are dry.
4. Plug the adaptor to the electric socket.
5. The cable has two sides, the USB A and USB C side. Plug the USB A side of the cable to the adaptor.
6. Plug the USB C side of the cable to your phone.
7. It is recommended to charge your phone at 40% until 80%.
