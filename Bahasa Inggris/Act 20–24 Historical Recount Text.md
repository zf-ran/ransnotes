# Act 20

> [!question] (1) How does the writer describe the Bubonic Plague?

The desease caused the sufferers to have swollen buboes (inflammation in the armpits, neck, or groins). The swellings may appear like the size of an egg which contains pus.

The victims usually had black tissue in their bodies. That's why the disease was also called the Black Death plague.

> [!question] (2) Why is it so described?

> [!question] (3) When did the plague begin?

In 1347, Europe.

> [!question] (4) How long did the plague last in Europe?

5 to 6 years.

> [!question] (5) Draw a chain of paths to describe how the Bubonic Plague arrived in Europe.

- Caused by *Yersinia pestis bacterium* and spread by fleas.
- The fleas inhabits the rodents' bodies as their hosts.
- When the host died, the fleas bit humans.
- The humans transmits the disease to others.
- The plague was spread by traders via the Silk Road.
- Come from Asia to Europe by the Mediterranean.
- It was identified in Crimea for the first time in 1347.
